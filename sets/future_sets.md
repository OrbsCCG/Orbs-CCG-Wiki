# Future Sets

When future sets are announced, this page will be updated with their latest information. You can expect new mechanics, 
themes, and a fresh draft format to be introduced with each new set.

Unlike the first set (Origins of Valor), future sets will tend to have just five colors plus one splash color, 
rather than six colors. This will make limited simpler by having fewer two color archetypes to consider while drafting.
At the same time, for constructed players, the sixth splash color will introduce enough new cards so that no
color feels neglected.
